Project overview

NoteBar is an AI-assisted note-taking system for personal knowledge management. My work supported a dataset for identifying concepts in notes written across different user scenarios.

My contributions

  • Designed personas and scenarios to guide synthetic note generation across different writing contexts.
  • Used Python and the OpenAI and Gemini APIs to support dataset generation.
  • Contributed to the team dataset of 16 personas, 3,173 notes, and 8,494 concept annotations.

Why it matters

The persona design translated differences in how people take notes into concrete inputs for building and evaluating a concept-labeling dataset.
