03 / COGNITIVE NEUROSCIENCE

Trust in AI Advice

An EEG/ERP experiment proposal

ROLE
Research proposal co-designer
CONTEXT
Cognitive neuroscience coursework · University of Rochester
STATUS
Experimental design proposal

Project overview

The proposed study asks how a source label changes decision revision, perceived reliability, and neural responses to outcome feedback.

My contributions

  • Co-designed a trial sequence with an initial decision, advice, a final decision, and outcome feedback.
  • Held advice content constant across the AI and human label conditions so the design could isolate the perceived source.
  • Proposed analyzing the P3b response to examine feedback processing alongside behavioral measures.

Why it matters

The design separates what participants are told about the source from the information they receive, making source attribution a question we can test experimentally.

  • Behavioral experiments
  • EEG / ERP
  • Source-label manipulation
  • P3b
