02 / BACKGROUND

About me

I’m a University of Rochester undergraduate studying Brain and Cognitive Sciences and Data Science, with an expected graduation in May 2027. I’m interested in what we can learn from the way people make decisions, adapt to new tools, and work through everyday problems.

My work spans designing behavioral studies, coding interviews, building datasets with language models, and automating tests for digital payment platforms. For graduate study, I want to use behavioral evidence to decide what to build and test whether the resulting systems support what people are trying to do.
