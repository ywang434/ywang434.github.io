01 / HUMAN–COMPUTER INTERACTION

FocusAdapt

Adaptive focus assistance in diminished reality

ROLE
Research assistant & manuscript co-author
CONTEXT
BEAR Lab · University of Rochester
STATUS
Manuscript submitted to ACM CHI 2027

Project overview

FocusAdapt explores how diminished reality could help people concentrate on a task by reducing the visibility of distracting objects while keeping relevant cues in view.

My contributions

  • Worked with a postdoctoral researcher on study design and contributed a cognitive science perspective on task-driven attention and object similarity.
  • Proposed a pre-task, multipoint eye-tracking calibration that was incorporated into the study protocol.
  • Prepared interview transcripts for approximately ten participants, coded responses using predefined categories, and compared sample coding with teammates to align interpretations.

Why it matters

This project connected design decisions about what to visually suppress with experimental decisions about how to measure attention and interpret participants’ experiences.
